Why Déjà Boo?The backstory

The experiments are borrowed from rock history. A real one ran for six nights in 1971, and it's been re-run a few times since. Tonight is the latest trial.

  1. FEB 18–24, 1971The Grateful Dead at the Capitol Theatre

    Six nights in Port Chester, NY. Each night about 2,000 fans were shown an image and asked to beam it to a man asleep in a dream lab 45 miles away. His dreams matched on 4 of 6 nights. Chance says 1.

  2. FEB 18–24, 2021Billy Strings: The Déjà Vu Experiment

    Same six dates, same theater, fifty years later, sent out by livestream.

  3. FEB 3–6, 2022Billy Strings: Déjà Tu

    Return to the Experiment. Four more nights at the Capitol Theatre.

  4. OCT 31, 2026The Déjà Boo Experiment

    118 Hillside Drive, Woodside. Billy's Halloween show plays in the den. Look for the posters from those nights in the hall.
