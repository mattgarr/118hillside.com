Dramatic Entry

Step through the iron-and-glass front doors and your eye goes straight through the house to the heritage oak in the backyard. The entry foyer has a cathedral ceiling, a starburst fixture, and a matching set of glass doors on the far wall.

Gathering Spaces

Exposed wood beams cross the living room’s vaulted ceiling, and large windows look out to the trees. Beyond it, the great room joins an all-white kitchen, a sitting area, and a dining room with a wall of glass to the terrace. The kitchen has quartz counters, a large peninsula, and a Bosch gas range and dishwasher. Downstairs, the bonus lounge has a refreshment counter, a beverage cooler, in-wall surround audio, and a 65-inch Samsung TV, with stacking glass doors that open to the rear terrace.

Room for Everyone

Six bedrooms and a dedicated skylit office leave space for kids’ bedrooms, home offices, guests, or a gym. The main level includes three bedrooms and a full bath with a Carrara marble-topped vanity. Downstairs are two more bedrooms, the skylit office, and a full bath with a dual-sink vanity and heated floors.

Primary Suite

The primary suite sits upstairs under a cathedral ceiling with four skylights, each with a remote-controlled shade. A loft sitting area with modern cable railings leads to the bedroom, which has a custom walk-in closet and stacking glass doors to a private balcony for watching sunsets over the western hills. The bath has heated floors, a dual-sink quartz vanity, a private skylit water closet, and a curbless shower for two.

Laundry and Storage

The main laundry room downstairs has a washer and dryer, and a main-level closet has hookups for a second washer and dryer. The oversized one-car garage adds high ceilings and built-in storage.

Outdoor Spaces for Relaxation and Adventure

Relax on the front porch, or entertain out back where the heritage oak anchors a paver-stone terrace with an outdoor kitchen, barbecue, and ceramic smoker under a pergola. RV hookups at the side of the home make the next road trip easy to plan.

A Day at Home

The house makes room for every part of the day: coffee in the loft under the skylights, homework at the kitchen peninsula, sunsets from the balcony, and movie nights in the bonus lounge.

The Heart of the Woodside Community

Located in the sought-after Woodside Elementary School District, the home is about a mile from the shops and restaurants of Woodside’s Town Center and miles of trails. It offers more than a place to live—it offers a place to belong.
