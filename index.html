Through the front doors

The front doors are iron and glass, and a matching pair stands on the far wall of the foyer. From the porch you look straight through the house to the heritage oak in the backyard. The foyer has a cathedral ceiling and a starburst light fixture.

The main rooms

Exposed wood beams cross the vaulted ceiling of the living room, and its large windows look out to the trees. Past it is the great room: an all-white kitchen, a sitting area, and a dining room with large windows that look out to the oak at the front of the house. The kitchen has quartz counters, a large peninsula, and a Bosch gas range and dishwasher.

Downstairs, the bonus lounge has a refreshment counter with a beverage cooler, in-wall surround audio and a 65-inch Samsung TV. Its stacking glass doors open to the rear terrace.

Bedrooms and an office

With six bedrooms and a separate skylit office, there is space to spare for guests, a second office or a gym. Three of the bedrooms are on the main level, along with a full bath that has a Carrara marble-topped vanity. Two more are downstairs with the office and a full bath with a dual-sink vanity and heated floors.

The primary suite

The primary suite is upstairs, under a cathedral ceiling with four skylights. Each skylight has a remote-controlled shade. You reach the bedroom through a loft sitting area with cable railings. The bedroom has a custom walk-in closet and stacking glass doors to a private balcony, which faces the sunset over the western hills.

The bath has heated floors, a dual-sink quartz vanity, a private skylit water closet and a curbless shower for two.

Laundry and storage

The main laundry room is downstairs and has a washer and dryer. A closet on the main level has hookups for a second washer and dryer. The oversized one-car garage has high ceilings and built-in storage.

Outside

There is a porch out front. Out back, the heritage oak stands over a paver-stone terrace with an outdoor kitchen, a barbecue and a ceramic smoker under a pergola. At the side of the house there are RV hookups.

Mornings, the loft under the skylights is a good place for coffee. In the evening the balcony faces the sunset over the western hills, and the bonus lounge downstairs is set up for a movie.

Life in Woodside Glens

The Glens is the hilly, wooded corner of Woodside between Cañada Road and Interstate 280. It has its own walking path along Glenwood Avenue, which the Town finished in 2024.

On Halloween the neighborhood closes its upper loop to cars. Neighbors host parties outside, and people from all over Woodside come to walk it.

  1. A short walk

    The upper loop

    Highland Terrace and Alta Mesa, where the neighborhood does its trick-or-treating on Halloween.

  2. About a mile

    Town Center

    Buck’s, The Village Bakery, The Village Pub, Roberts Market and the library, all along Woodside Road.

  3. A little over a mile

    Woodside Elementary

    The public school for kindergarten through eighth grade, on Woodside Road just past the library. GreatSchools rates it 10 out of 10 as of November 2026.

  4. A few minutes by car

    Up the hill

    Huddart and Wunderlich, two county parks with redwoods and more than 40 miles of trails between them, and the Woodside Store, a general store from 1854 that is now a free museum.
