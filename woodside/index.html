Life in Woodside Glens

118 Hillside Drive is in the Glens, the hilly, wooded corner of Woodside between Cañada Road and Interstate 280. This page starts at the front door and works outward, nearest things first.

Halloween in the Glens

On Halloween, the upper loop is closed to cars.

That evening the Glens shuts its upper loop, around Highland Terrace and Alta Mesa, and hands it to people on foot. Neighbors host parties outside. People come from all over Woodside to walk the loop and trick-or-treat. It is a short walk from the front door of 118 Hillside Drive.

The school district lists it among its community events as “the annual trick or treating on Halloween in the Glens” and promises “a spooktacular evening filled with music, food and no shortage of candy.” Its advice is to bring jackets and “lots of flashlights, it gets very, very dark.” In 2026 it ran from 5:30 to 7:30.

At the front door

The Glens

Hillside Drive comes off Glenwood Avenue, the street the Glens is centered on. The neighborhood sits on a wooded hill between Cañada Road and Interstate 280. It was first developed in the 1920s, as vacation homes for San Franciscans, and it is still a part of Woodside where you can walk to a neighbor’s house.

The Town’s own website sums up Woodside as “Trails, not sidewalks. Hitching posts, not stop lights.” The Glens has something close to a sidewalk anyway. The Glens Path is a walking path along Glenwood Avenue that the Town finished in 2024, with a ribbon cutting near the corner of Cañada Road.

About a mile

Favorite spots

Glenwood Avenue comes out on Cañada Road, and Cañada Road ends at Woodside Road in the middle of Town Center. The Town’s general plan lists a paved pedestrian path and bike lanes along Cañada Road. From the house it is about a mile.

In Town Center

  • Buck’s of Woodside

    3062 Woodside Road

    Breakfast, lunch and dinner since 1991. The restaurant calls its dining room “half restaurant, half living museum,” and it is hung with folk art, model planes and cowboy boots.

  • The Village Bakery & Cafe

    3052 Woodside Road, across from Roberts Market

    A bakery for bread and pastries, joined to a restaurant with a full bar. It opens at 7 in the morning.

  • Woodside Library

    3140 Woodside Road

    A branch of San Mateo County Libraries. There is a California native plant garden tucked away in back.

  • The Village Pub

    2967 Woodside Road

    The name undersells it. This is a fine-dining restaurant, and it holds one star in the 2026 Michelin Guide.

  • Roberts Market

    3015 Woodside Road

    The town grocery, with a deli, on the corner where Cañada Road and Mountain Home Road meet Woodside Road.

  • Town Hall and the museum

    2955 and 2961 Woodside Road

    Independence Hall, built in 1884, stands next to Town Hall and is used for Town meetings and events. The Woodside Community Museum is beside them.

A few minutes by car

  • The Woodside Store

    3300 Tripp Road, at Kings Mountain Road

    Built in 1854, it was Dr. Tripp’s general store, post office and dental office. The county keeps it as a free museum, stocked the way it was in the 1880s. It is open Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10 to 4 and weekends from noon to 4.

  • Huddart Park

    1100 Kings Mountain Road

    A county park of 973 acres with 24 miles of trails, a redwood forest, and picnic areas with barbecue pits and a playground. Parking is $6.

  • Wunderlich Park

    4040 Woodside Road

    A county park of 942 acres with 17 miles of trails for hikers and riders, and the historic Folger Stable. Parking is free.

  • Filoli

    86 Cañada Road

    A historic house with 16 acres of formal gardens on a 654-acre estate, owned by the National Trust for Historic Preservation and open to visitors. It is north of the Glens on Cañada Road.

A little over a mile

The school

Woodside Elementary School is at 3195 Woodside Road, just past the library. It is public, and it is the Woodside Elementary School District’s one campus, with transitional kindergarten through eighth grade.

GreatSchools rates it 10 out of 10, as of November 2026.

A pedestrian path runs along Woodside Road between Roberts Market and the school.

Each spring the town closes Woodside Road for May Day. There is a fun run that starts at the library, a parade down the road, a maypole dance, and a carnival at the school. The Almanac counted 2026 as the 104th year.

This house is listed in the Woodside Elementary School District. Districts set their own boundaries and enrollment rules, so buyers should confirm attendance for this address with the district. The same goes for high school, where Woodside is part of the Sequoia Union High School District.

A short drive

Trails and open space

The Town keeps a public system of equestrian and pedestrian trails, and its Trails Committee looks after it. Riders and walkers share it. Huddart and Wunderlich are the two big parks close by, and there are two more places worth knowing about.

Edgewood Park and Natural Preserve is north of the Glens, off Edgewood Road. It is 467 acres of woodlands and grasslands, and the county says the grasslands are known for their wildflowers each spring.

Cañada Road on Sundays. From 9 to 3 on Sundays the county closes 3.8 miles of Cañada Road to cars, from the Filoli entrance north to Highway 92, for people on bikes and on foot. It is the same road that runs past the Glens. Holiday weekends are skipped.

Farther out

The roads out

Interstate 280 crosses Woodside Road just east of Town Center, a few minutes from the house by car. Going south, the next exit is Sand Hill Road, for Menlo Park and Stanford, and Page Mill Road is the exit for Palo Alto. Woodside Road is also Highway 84, and past the freeway it runs on into Redwood City.

Cañada College is just north of the Glens, also a few minutes away by car. It is one of the three community colleges in the San Mateo County Community College District.
